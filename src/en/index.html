Before you install

What it can't do, up front.

Sunset Talk is narrow on purpose. Please check these first.

  • {{icon:wifi-off}}

    Same Wi-Fi only

    It doesn't connect over mobile data or across different networks. You can't chat with someone far away.

  • {{icon:alert}}

    Guest and public Wi-Fi often block it

    Cafés, hotels, event venues and company guest networks often stop devices from seeing each other. An iPhone Personal Hotspot may not work for now either. Use your office or home router.

  • {{icon:bell-off}}

    Both of you keep the app open

    You both need Sunset Talk installed and open while you chat. With no server, there are no push notifications for new messages.

  • {{icon:eye-off}}

    Your nickname is visible on the Wi-Fi

    While the app is open, devices on the same Wi-Fi can see your nickname, your avatar and this phone's local IP address. They can't see your messages.

  • {{icon:users}}

    One-to-one only

    There are no group chats yet. You talk with one person at a time.

  • {{icon:key}}

    No forward secrecy yet

    If the keys stored on a phone leak, someone who recorded your past traffic could decrypt it. The app hasn't had an outside security audit yet.

Security, in plain words

Only the two phones can read the messages.

No inflated claims. Just what the app actually does.

  • {{icon:check}}End-to-end encrypted by default

    Messages are encrypted on the sending phone and only decrypted on the receiving one. Someone snooping on the Wi-Fi can't read them.

  • {{icon:check}}Check it's really them

    When you first connect, both screens show a fingerprint (a set of numbers). Read them to each other and tap confirm if they match. Messages wait until you do.

  • {{icon:check}}History on your two phones, encrypted

    Chat history is stored only on the two phones, encrypted with a device key. You can wipe this phone's data in one step from Settings.

  • {{icon:check}}No ad or analytics tools

    The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. It sends nothing to us.

Details for experts Cryptography, what the LAN can see, what we don't do
Key agreementX25519 ECDH with the static identity key. Session keys come from HKDF-SHA256 bound to the full transcript of both hello messages; both sides must exchange an HMAC-SHA256 key confirmation before the connection completes, so tampering with version or capability negotiation fails there.
MessagesAES-256-GCM with a random 12-byte nonce per message. Each message carries a SHA-256 hash chain link (prevHash), compared in constant time; the last 2,048 message IDs are remembered to drop duplicates.
Handshake replay16-byte nonce and a ±120 s timestamp window. Wrong-length, all-zero and low-order-point public keys are rejected.
Peer verificationTrust on first use, plus a manual fingerprint comparison: the first 64 bits (16 digits) of SHA-256 of the public key. A changed key asks for verification again, and sending, receiving and sync wait until it's done.
StorageMessage bodies are encrypted with a device key (AES-256-GCM) in the app's SQLite database. Keys live in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage and the iOS keychain (this device only). Android backup is off.
Visible on the LAN in plaintextThe mDNS (DNS-SD) announcement: nickname, avatar, peer ID, ports, local IPv4 addresses and protocol version. Also each frame's type, sequence number, time and size, and keep-alive signals.
Not doneForward secrecy, key rotation, message signatures (non-repudiation), screenshot blocking. No outside security audit yet. Rooted or jailbroken devices and malware on the device are out of scope.
FAQ

Questions

Is there really no server?

Right. In this version we run no server at all. Phones find each other with local network announcements (mDNS) inside your router, and the chat is a direct connection between the two phones. So we have no way to receive who talks to whom, or what was said.

Do I need internet?

You need to be on Wi-Fi. The chat only travels between the two phones inside the router and doesn't go out to the internet.

Why only on the same Wi-Fi?

That's the design. Connecting only inside one router means no server is needed to relay or store your chat. It's also why the chat stops when you leave.

I can't see the other person.

Check that both phones are on the same Wi-Fi (same network name) and both have the app open. Guest and public networks often block devices from seeing each other. On iPhone, Local Network access must be on.

See the checklist

What if I get an invite from someone I don't know?

Nothing starts unless you accept. Decline it, then long-press that person in the list to block or report them. Reports are kept only on your phone, and you can email them to us if you want.

Where is my chat history kept?

Only on the two phones in the chat, encrypted with each phone's device key. "Delete all data" in Settings wipes this phone's profile, keys and history in one step. The other phone's copy has to be deleted there.

Can more than two people chat?

Not yet. Chats are one-to-one.

Does it cost anything?

This version is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
